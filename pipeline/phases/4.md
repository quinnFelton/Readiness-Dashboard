Build against the connection framework and the scoring engine already on main.

- Rate-limit awareness: parse X-RateLimit-Usage/Limit and back off before the ceiling.
- Training-load heuristic per PLAN §5.2, formula documented in a comment.
Tests: webhook challenge, create/update/delete events, short/non-ride activities skipped, refresh flow, retry produces one row, rate-limit backoff.
